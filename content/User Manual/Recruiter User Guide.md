# Introduction

ResuMint is an Artificial Intelligence powered recruitment platform designed to simplify the hiring process through intelligent resume analysis, candidate evaluation, and automated recruitment tools.

The platform enables recruiters to identify qualified candidates faster while reducing manual screening and improving hiring decisions.

- Talent Acquisition Specialists

- Recruitment Agencies

# Getting Started

## Creating a Recruiter Account

1. Open the ResuMint platform.
2. Select **Create Account**.
3. Choose **Recruiter Account**.
4. Enter the required information.
5. Create a secure password.
6. Verify your email address.
7. Sign in.

Result

Your recruiter account is successfully created.

---

## Signing In

1. Open the Login page.
2. Enter your registered email address.
3. Enter your password.
4. Select **Login**.

Result

The Recruiter Dashboard is displayed.

---

## Resetting Your Password

1. Select **Forgot Password**.
2. Enter your registered email address.
3. Follow the instructions provided.
4. Create a new password.
5. Sign in using your updated credentials.

# Setting Up Your Recruiter Profile

Purpose

Your recruiter profile provides candidates with information about your organization and helps establish credibility.

Steps

1. Open **Profile Settings**.
2. Enter your organization details.
3. Add company information.
4. Save your changes.

Result

Your organization profile is available for candidates to view.

---

# Creating a Job Posting

Purpose

Create a vacancy for candidates to apply for.

Steps

1. Open the Dashboard.
2. Select **Create Job Posting**.
3. Enter the job title.
4. Provide a detailed job description.
5. Specify the required skills.
6. Add qualifications and experience requirements.
7. Review the information.
8. Select **Publish**.

Result

The job posting becomes available to candidates.

---

# Managing Job Postings

Recruiters can:

- Edit job postings.
- Update job requirements.
- Monitor applications.
- Close filled positions.
- Reopen vacancies when required.

---

# Reviewing Applications

Applications are automatically organized within the Recruiter Dashboard.

To review applications:

1. Open the relevant job posting.
2. Navigate to **Applications**.
3. Review submitted resumes.
4. View candidate profiles.
5. Review Candidate Job Fit.
6. Examine AI-generated recommendations.

Result

Applications are available for evaluation.

---

# Understanding Smart Semantic Scan

Purpose

Smart Semantic Scan automatically compares candidate resumes against the requirements of a job posting.

The analysis evaluates:

- Skills
- Qualifications
- Work Experience
- Industry Relevance
- Job Requirements

Benefits

- Faster screening
- Improved candidate matching
- Reduced manual review
- More accurate recruitment decisions

---

# Understanding Candidate Job Fit

Purpose

Candidate Job Fit measures how closely a candidate matches the requirements of a specific role.

The AI evaluates:

- Skills Match
- Education
- Experience
- Resume Relevance
- Overall Compatibility

High Match

Candidates with a high score may be suitable for shortlisting.

Lower Match

Candidates with lower scores may benefit from improving their resumes before applying for similar opportunities.

---

# Troubleshooting

## Unable to Create a Job Posting

Check that:

- All required fields are completed.
- Your internet connection is active.
- The page is refreshed before trying again.

---

## Candidate Job Fit Not Displayed

Possible causes:

- No applications have been submitted.
- Resume analysis is still processing.

Refresh the page after a few minutes.

---

## Micro-Assessments Not Generated

Ensure that:

- The job posting includes sufficient role requirements.
- Candidate analysis has completed.

---

## Unable to View Candidate Rankings

Check:

- Applications have been received.
- Resume analysis has completed.
- Internet connectivity is stable.

# Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can I edit a published job posting?

A: Yes.

Recruiters can edit existing vacancies from the Recruiter Dashboard.

---

Q: Can I reopen a closed job posting?

Yes.

Closed vacancies may be reopened if recruitment resumes.

---

Q: What does Candidate Job Fit represent?

Candidate Job Fit indicates how closely a candidate's qualifications and experience align with the requirements of a specific job posting.

---

Q: Are Explainable Scores available for every applicant?

Scores are generated after candidate analysis is complete.
