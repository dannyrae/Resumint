# Introduction

ResuMint is an Artificial Intelligence powered recruitment platform that helps candidates improve their resumes, evaluate job compatibility, identify missing skills, and prepare for interviews.

The platform provides personalized recommendations to help candidates increase their chances of securing employment.

---

# Who Should Use This Guide

This guide is intended for:

- Students
- Graduates
- Entry-Level Professionals
- Experienced Professionals
- Job Seekers
- Career Changers

# Getting Started

## Creating an Account

1. Open the ResuMint platform.
2. Select **Create Account**.
3. Choose **Candidate Account**.
4. Enter your personal information.
5. Create a secure password.
6. Verify your email address.
7. Log in to access your dashboard.

Result

Your candidate account is successfully created.

---

## Signing In

1. Open the Login page.
2. Enter your registered email address.
3. Enter your password.
4. Select **Login**.

Result

The Candidate Dashboard is displayed.

---

## Resetting Your Password

1. Select **Forgot Password**.
2. Enter your registered email address.
3. Follow the instructions sent to your email.
4. Create a new password.
5. Sign in using your new credentials.

# Uploading a Resume

Purpose

Upload your resume for AI analysis.

Steps

1. Open the Resume section.
2. Select **Upload Resume**.
3. Choose a PDF or DOCX file.
4. Confirm the upload.

Result

Your resume is uploaded and prepared for AI analysis.

Supported Formats

- PDF
- DOCX

---

# Uploading a Job Description

Purpose

Compare your resume with a specific job opportunity.

Steps

1. Open the Job Description section.
2. Select **Upload Job Description**.
3. Upload the job description or enter the required information.
4. Confirm the upload.

Result

The job description becomes available for comparison.

---

# Resume Match Score

Purpose

Measure how closely your resume matches a selected job.

How It Works

The AI compares your resume against the uploaded job description and generates a compatibility score.

The analysis considers:

- Skills
- Qualifications
- Work Experience
- Keywords
- Role Relevance

Result

A Resume Match Score is displayed along with recommendations for improvement.

---

# ATS Compatibility Score

Purpose

Evaluate how effectively your resume can be processed by Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS).

The analysis reviews:

- Resume formatting
- Section headings
- Keywords
- Readability
- Structure

Result

An ATS Compatibility Score is generated together with optimization suggestions.

# Troubleshooting

## Unable to Upload Resume

Possible causes:

- Unsupported file format
- Corrupted file
- Poor internet connection

---

## Resume Match Score Not Displayed

Possible causes:

- No job description uploaded
- Resume analysis still processing

---

## ATS Score Missing

Wait for the analysis to complete and refresh the page.

---

## Unable to Apply for Jobs

Check:

- Internet connection
- Resume upload status
- Account verification

# Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Which file formats are supported?

A:

- PDF
- DOCX

---

Q: Can I upload a new version of my resume?

Yes.

You may replace your resume whenever necessary.

---

Q: Does a high Resume Match Score guarantee employment?

No.

The score is intended to help you understand how well your resume aligns with a specific role. Hiring decisions remain the responsibility of employers.

---

Q: How often should I update my resume?

Update your resume whenever you gain new skills, certifications, education, or work experience.
